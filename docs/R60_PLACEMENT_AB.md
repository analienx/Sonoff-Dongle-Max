# R60 bounded coordinator placement A/B (2026-09-22)

Goal: distinguish a placement-sensitive RF problem from persistent dense-neighbor/route problems without changing Zigbee firmware, TX, channel, identity or pairings. This is a short **diagnostic**, not proof of permanently flawless service.

- Run `py -3 deploy/r60_placement_ab.py before --seconds 180` **before moving** the production SONOFF, using existing Z2M owner. Save the private result on the authorized laptop. The script selects up to 12 powered routers with read-accessible `state`, across at least four named rooms; intentionally relay-disconnected bedroom bulbs are excluded.
- Physically move the whole dongle/antenna away from Pi/USB3 SSD and USB3 cabling. Leave other Zigbee/Wi-Fi settings and cabling unchanged if possible. No network reset or re-pairing.
- Run `py -3 deploy/r60_placement_ab.py after --seconds 180`, then `py -3 deploy/r60_placement_ab.py report`. The `after` stage freezes the **exact** sampled device names from `before`; a missing or unsupported device is a failure, not silently replaced.
- Each stage reads non-clearing NCP counters twice through the existing owner, requests one read-only `state` per router sequentially, and records fresh non-retained MQTT responses with per-device latency/timeout. Timeouts count. No generic pings, device power switching, mesh map, second serial owner, HA log collection, flash or network reconfiguration.
- Report MAC failure fraction/rate, neighbor add+remove rate, CCA failures, traffic comparability, per-device response/failure and p50 latency. Reject owner restarts, negative counter deltas (hourly reset/wrap), too few MAC outcomes, changed device cohort or insufficient room coverage. Do not treat empty route-error logs, a lone successful bulb, or a short window as network-wide recovery.
- A fresh non-retained state after a `/get` is a practical delivery **proxy**, not cryptographic proof that the response came from that exact request: unsolicited reporting may coincide. Separate room labels do not prove independent RF routes. Physical movement changes both local USB3 coupling and antenna geometry, so improvement cannot uniquely attribute USB3 noise.
- All raw device names, IDs and per-device results remain in `C:\Workspace\.analienx\sonoff-private\`; no secrets or private neighbor rows are committed. Temporary Z2M owner extension is automatically removed by existing typed-gate rollback. If a stage fails, do not auto-retry or report a pass; inspect the failed stage and extension-cleanup status first.

## Known intentionally unpowered routers (2026-09-22)

User reports Workroom left/right dimmers, bedroom bulbs and LivingRoom circle lights are generally turned off at the mains. Exclude these by design from both A/B device denominators. The saved BEFORE raw result remains immutable, but its one WorkroomTableRightDimmer timeout must NOT be counted as a confirmed Zigbee delivery failure. The AFTER cohort is the same BEFORE cohort minus the excluded target, not a newly selected favorable sample. Remaining unknown-power nonresponses are inconclusive unless power was verified. MAC and churn baseline remain unchanged. The fixed cohort retains 11 devices across 5 zones; physical RF hop independence is unproven.
