# R60 bounded coordinator placement A/B (2026-09-22)

Goal: distinguish a placement-sensitive RF problem from persistent dense-neighbor/route problems without changing Zigbee firmware, TX, channel, identity or pairings. This is a short **diagnostic**, not proof of permanently flawless service.

- Run `py -3 deploy/r60_placement_ab.py before --seconds 180` **before moving** the production SONOFF, using existing Z2M owner. Save the private result on the authorized laptop. The script selects up to 12 powered routers with read-accessible `state`, across at least four named rooms; intentionally relay-disconnected bedroom bulbs are excluded.
- Physically move the whole dongle/antenna away from Pi/USB3 SSD and USB3 cabling. Leave other Zigbee/Wi-Fi settings and cabling unchanged if possible. No network reset or re-pairing.
- Run `py -3 deploy/r60_placement_ab.py after --seconds 180`, then `py -3 deploy/r60_placement_ab.py report`. The `after` stage freezes the **exact** sampled device names from `before`; a missing or unsupported device is recorded as a per-device non-response, not silently replaced or used to abort the whole sample.
- Each stage reads non-clearing NCP counters twice through the existing owner, requests one read-only `state` per router sequentially, and records fresh non-retained MQTT responses with per-device latency/timeout. Timeouts count. No generic pings, device power switching, mesh map, second serial owner, HA log collection, flash or network reconfiguration.
- Report MAC failure fraction/rate, neighbor add+remove rate, CCA failures, traffic comparability, per-device response/failure and p50 latency. Reject owner restarts, negative counter deltas, too few MAC outcomes, changed device cohort, or insufficient room coverage. Additionally, inspect *only* matching clear markers in the exact container log interval locally on HA; reject a window if the hourly NCP counter clear is detected, even if its values have already re-accumulated. If that narrow audit fails, classify as inconclusive. The old pre-audit baseline is audited retrospectively during the after stage; no overwrite or additional full log capture. Do not treat empty route-error logs, a lone successful bulb, or a short window as network-wide recovery.
- A fresh non-retained state after a `/get` is a practical delivery **proxy**, not cryptographic proof that the response came from that exact request: unsolicited reporting may coincide. Separate room labels do not prove independent RF routes. Physical movement changes both local USB3 coupling and antenna geometry, so improvement cannot uniquely attribute USB3 noise.
- All raw device names, IDs and per-device results remain in `C:\Workspace\.analienx\sonoff-private\`; no secrets or private neighbor rows are committed. Temporary Z2M owner extension is automatically removed by existing typed-gate rollback. If a stage fails, do not auto-retry or report a pass; inspect the failed stage and extension-cleanup status first.

## Known intentionally unpowered routers (2026-09-22)

User reports Workroom left/right dimmers, bedroom bulbs and LivingRoom circle lights are generally turned off at the mains. Exclude these by design from both A/B device denominators. The saved BEFORE raw result remains immutable, but its one WorkroomTableRightDimmer timeout must NOT be counted as a confirmed Zigbee delivery failure. The AFTER cohort is the same BEFORE cohort minus the excluded target, not a newly selected favorable sample. Remaining unknown-power nonresponses are inconclusive unless power was verified. MAC and churn baseline remain unchanged. The fixed cohort retains 11 devices across 5 zones; physical RF hop independence is unproven.

## Code-review limitations and result interpretation

- The old baseline contained twelve read targets, but WorkroomTableRightDimmer is generally switched off. The paired report excludes this device from BOTH stages, and lists it separately. Workroom left/right dimmers, bedroom relay-switched bulbs and living-room circle lights are excluded up front. A missing response from any other router is not necessarily a mesh fault until its mains power is confirmed.
- The stock Zigbee2MQTT `/get` interface does not expose a request-specific ZCL sequence or APS acknowledgement to this MQTT client. A new non-retained `state` message is a **response proxy**, not a verified ZCL transaction; unrelated spontaneous reports can coincide. Do not advertise 11/11 as 100% ZCL success.
- Each router is sampled once per placement: this is a controlled spot check and cannot prove flawless long-term mesh operation. Require meaningful improvement in MAC failure fraction and neighbor turnover with comparable traffic, and no device-level regression. A three-minute window is a quick discriminator, not a proof that one RF cause has been isolated.
- Two full NCP diagnostic extension save/read/remove cycles per phase (four per A/B) are more overhead than strictly necessary, but preserve the reviewed one-shot rollback contract. Do not bypass the typed gate or hold an unreviewed extension open to save a few seconds.
- If hourly-clear provenance or active power state cannot be verified, the report must say provisional/inconclusive. Do not fabricate a success for missing data.
